The application checklist

The five items in Annex A

1
A sworn statement
Or a joint sworn statement for a cloud or outsourced system, describing the system, your invoices and the reports it produces.
2
Sample invoices
Printouts of your invoices and supplementary documents, as the system generates them.
3
Sample books and reports
Your books of accounts and the other system reports.
4
An audit trail printout
Printed from the system, showing who did what and when.
5
The signed requirements checklist
The Annex B requirements. Ask your RDO for its current templates, as annex labels differ between issuances.
