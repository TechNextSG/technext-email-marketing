Is your accounting system registered with the BIR?

If you are not sure, start there. The BIR registers your business’s use of a system at your Revenue District Office (RDO). It does not register a software brand, so buying “registered” software is not the same as being registered.

With our 5 Day Express BIR CAS Set-up*, we set Odoo up for BIR CAS requirements as a plug-in to your current system, as quick as 5 days.

At a glance

BIR CAS registration in four lines

What a CAS isA system that records your transactions, generates your invoices and produces your books of accounts and BIR reports.
What you registerYour business’s use of a system, at your own RDO. Head office and branches each count.
Who decidesYour RDO, after it reviews your application.
Every yearComputerised books are registered online in ORUS. For 2026 books: by 30 January 2027 (calendar-year taxpayers).
