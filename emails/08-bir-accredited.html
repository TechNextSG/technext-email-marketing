Was your software sold as “BIR‑accredited”?

Be careful with that claim. The BIR registers your business’s use of a system. It does not approve software brands, and it has said so in public.

The short answer

No software is BIR-accredited

What the BIR registersYour business’s use of a system. Not a software brand.
14 September 2026In a public advisory, the BIR said it has not accredited, certified or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider, and warned against claims such as “BIR-accredited”.
RMC 110-2026Dated 12 October 2026. It sets the accreditation rules for e-invoicing service providers. No list had been published when we wrote this.
 

What to look for instead

✓Does it meet the Annex B requirements: an audit trail, posted entries voided and never edited, system-generated invoice numbers, user stamps, report headers with TIN and branch code, exports, unique user IDs, backups?
✓Can it produce the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail your RDO asks for?
✓Will your business receive its own Acknowledgement Certificate (AC) from your RDO?
