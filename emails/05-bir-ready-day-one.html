Registering Odoo as your CAS

BIR CAS registration: who does what

We set Odoo up as your Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA), and prepare the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail printout your application needs. Your business files at its Revenue District Office.

What you register
Your use of a system, at your RDO, not a software brand
Who decides
The BIR, through your RDO. Not TechNext.

What the BIR checks

Requirements your RDO looks for, set up in Odoo

✓  Audit trail, printable and protected
✓  Posted entries voided, never edited
✓  System-generated invoice numbers
✓  User stamp on every record
✓  Report headers with TIN and branch code
✓  CSV or DAT exports; 2306, 2307, 2316
✓  Unique user IDs and logged changes
✓  Backups kept for ten years
A word on “BIR-accredited” software
There is no such thing. The BIR registers how your business uses a system, not a software brand, and its 14 September 2026 advisory says it has not accredited any e-invoicing provider. You register Odoo as your computerised books at your Revenue District Office, and we prepare the samples, audit trail and system description with you.
