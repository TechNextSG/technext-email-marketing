What we set up

The modules that carry a business

Sales & CRM
Every inquiry in one pipeline with an owner and a next step. Quotes out the same day.
POS & Inventory
Counter sales that move real stock, with reorder rules instead of end-of-week surprises.
Accounting & GST
Singapore chart of accounts, 9% GST codes and reporting, bank reconciliation that runs itself.
Purchasing & Suppliers

HR & Payroll

Projects & Field Service
Jobs, milestones and site visits that bill themselves when the work is signed off.
Singapore · e-invoicing
InvoiceNow is coming to GST-registered businesses
IRAS is phasing in a requirement for GST-registered businesses to transmit invoice data through InvoiceNow, Singapore's Peppol-based e-invoicing network, with existing registrants brought in by turnover band. Odoo can send on that network, and we set it up with your chart of accounts and GST codes rather than leaving it as a switch you discover later. Read the IRAS requirement.
