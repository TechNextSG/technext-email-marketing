Who is covered

Must issue e-invoices by 31 December 2026

✓ Small, medium and large taxpayers selling through e-commerce or internet transactions
✓ Taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Service, and large taxpayers under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act
✓ Businesses on CAS or CBA with e-invoicing or other invoicing software
– Micro taxpayers are exempt.

Not sure which group you are in? Check with your accountant or your Revenue District Office. We do this with you on the first call.

A word of caution
Be careful with “BIR-accredited” claims
In its 14 September 2026 advisory, the BIR said it has not accredited or endorsed any software or e-invoicing provider. Your PTI and CAS applications are still filed with the BIR in your company’s name. We tell you plainly what Odoo does, what we build, and what you still file.
