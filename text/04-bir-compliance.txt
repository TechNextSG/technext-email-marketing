AT A GLANCE

- Deadline: 31 December 2026
- First step: a Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice
- Then: EIS Certification within six months of the PTI
- Applies to: head office and every branch


WHERE BIR COMPLIANCE HURTS TODAY

- Invoices that can't be sent - if your system can't transmit sales
  data to the BIR, it needs work before the deadline.
- 2307s typed by hand - Odoo 20 generates BIR 2306 and 2307
  certificates straight from your vendor bills.
- Month-end in Excel - SLSP, SAWT and QAP come from the same entries as
  your books, with DAT exports for the BIR's tools.
- CAS paperwork - set Odoo up first, and your sample outputs come from
  the system you will actually use.
- Branches doing it differently - the rule covers head office and every
  branch. One Odoo database gives them one invoice flow.
- Digging for an audit - posted entries keep their history and
  attachments, so what an examiner asks for is already together.


WHO MUST ISSUE E-INVOICES BY 31 DECEMBER 2026

- Small, medium and large taxpayers selling through e-commerce or
  internet transactions
- Taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Service, and large taxpayers
  under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act
- Businesses on CAS or CBA with e-invoicing or other invoicing software
- Micro taxpayers are exempt.

Not sure which group you are in? Check with your accountant or your
