Who should claim a slot

You must issue e-invoices by 31 December 2026 if you are

A small, medium or large taxpayer selling through e-commerce or internet
transactions

Under the Large Taxpayers Service, or a large taxpayer under the Ease of
Paying Taxes Act

On CAS or CBA with e-invoicing or other invoicing software

Micro taxpayers are exempt. Under RR 26-2025 and RMC 98-2026. Your
accountant or Revenue District Office confirms which group you are in.
