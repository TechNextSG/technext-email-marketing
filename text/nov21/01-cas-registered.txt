IS YOUR ACCOUNTING SYSTEM REGISTERED WITH THE BIR?

If you are not sure, start there. The BIR registers your business's use of a system at your Revenue District Office (RDO). It does not register a software brand, so buying "registered" software is not the same as being registered.
