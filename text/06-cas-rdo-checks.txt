Would your system pass the RDO's checks?

Worth knowing before you file. The RDO evaluates your system after it issues

finds gaps, it can revoke the certificate. So check first.

What the RDO looks at
A sample of the requirements checklist (CAS requirements checklist, sample):
- Audit trail, printable and protected
- Posted entries voided, never edited
- System-generated invoice numbers
- User stamp on every record
- Report headers with TIN and branch code
- CSV or DAT exports; 2306, 2307, 2316
- Unique user IDs and logged changes
- Backups kept for ten years
