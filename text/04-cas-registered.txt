What a CAS is
A Computerized Accounting System (CAS) records your transactions, generates
your invoices and produces your books of accounts and BIR reports.

What you register: Your use of a system, at your RDO, not a software brand
What you receive: An Acknowledgement Certificate (AC) with an AC control
number
How long: Per RMC 5-2021, three working days after a complete application;
the RDO decides
Every year: Computerised books registered online in ORUS

  Permit to Use.
