The short answer
No software is BIR-accredited:
What the BIR registers: Your business's use of a system. Not a software brand.
14 September 2026: In a public advisory, the BIR said it has not accredited,
certified or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider, and warned against
claims such as "BIR-accredited".
RMC 110-2026: Dated 12 October 2026. It sets the accreditation rules for
e-invoicing service providers. No list had been published when we wrote
this.

What to look for instead:
- Does it meet the Annex B requirements: an audit trail, posted entries
  voided and never edited, system-generated invoice numbers, user stamps,
  report headers with TIN and branch code, exports, unique user IDs,
  backups?
- Can it produce the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail your
  RDO asks for?
- Will your business receive its own Acknowledgement Certificate (AC) from
  your RDO?
