CAS or CBA
CAS (Computerized Accounting System):
- What it covers: The full cycle, from the transaction and the invoice to
  the financial reports
- Invoices: System-generated
- Who must use it: Large taxpayers must maintain or use a CAS (RR 9-2009,
  restated in RMO 9-2021)
- Cloud set-ups (both): Registered too. Odoo Online or Odoo.sh needs a joint
  sworn statement with the provider that the system cannot suppress sales.

CBA (Computerized Books of Accounts):
- What it covers: Books of accounts only: journals, ledger, sales and
  purchase books, inventory book
- Invoices: Issued manually (a system that issues invoices by hand counts as
  a CBA)
- Who must use it: Optional for non-large taxpayers, who may choose a CAS or
  a CBA
