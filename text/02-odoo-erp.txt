THE MODULES THAT CARRY A BUSINESS

The first phase usually covers Accounting, Sales and Inventory. The rest
follow on the same database as you need them.

- Sales & CRM - every enquiry in one pipeline with an owner and a next
  step, so none goes cold.
- POS & Inventory - counter sales that move real stock, with reorder
  rules instead of end-of-week surprises.
- Accounting, GST & BIR - set up for GST in Singapore and BIR
  requirements in the Philippines (2307, SLSP, SAWT, QAP), with bank
  sync and reconciliation.
- Purchasing & Suppliers - purchase orders tied to the sale that needed
  them, and supplier prices that stay current.
- HR & Payroll - leave, expenses and timesheets in one place, out of a
  spreadsheet nobody trusts.
- Projects & Field Service - jobs, milestones and site visits invoiced
  from the work that was signed off.


SINGAPORE: INVOICENOW IS BEING PHASED IN FOR GST-REGISTERED BUSINESSES

Under the GST InvoiceNow Requirement, GST-registered businesses must
send invoice data to IRAS through InvoiceNow, Singapore's Peppol-based
network. Existing registrants are phased in by total annual supplies,
from April 2028 to April 2031. We set up Odoo for GST, and for
InvoiceNow through the Access Point you choose, as part of your
Accounting set-up rather than a switch you discover later.

HOW AN IMPLEMENTATION RUNS - FOUR STEPS, STANDARD ODOO FIRST

01. Discovery - we map how orders, stock and money move today, with the
    people who run them. You leave with a process map, a written scope
    and a quotation.
02. Configuration & data migration - on a staging copy, never your live
    database: standard Odoo first, then your customers, products and
    open balances cleaned, loaded and reconciled.
03. Training & integration - training by role, on your own data, so
    people learn their own screens. Then banks, payments, stores and
    email connected.
04. Go-live and support - a controlled cut-over, the first month-end
    done together, then support for fixes, upgrades and small changes.
