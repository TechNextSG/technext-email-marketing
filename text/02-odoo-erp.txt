THE MODULES THAT CARRY A BUSINESS

Start with the two that hurt most. Add the rest when the first ones are
earning their keep.

- Sales & CRM - every inquiry in one pipeline with an owner and a next
  step. Quotes out the same day.
- POS & Inventory - counter sales that move real stock, with reorder
  rules instead of end-of-week surprises.
- Accounting & GST - Singapore chart of accounts, 9% GST codes and
  reporting, bank reconciliation that runs itself.
- Purchasing & Suppliers - purchase orders tied to the sale that needed
  them, and supplier prices that stay current.
- HR & Payroll - leave, claims and timesheets off WhatsApp and out of a
  spreadsheet nobody trusts.
- Projects & Field Service - jobs, milestones and site visits that bill
  themselves when the work is signed off.
