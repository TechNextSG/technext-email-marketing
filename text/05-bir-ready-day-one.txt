Straight talk, no "BIR-accredited" claims

No software is BIR-accredited. The BIR registers your business's use of
a system, and its 14 September 2026 advisory says it has not accredited
any e-invoicing provider. Your business registers Odoo as its
computerised books and files at its Revenue District Office. We prepare
the samples, audit trail and system description with you.
