A word on "BIR-accredited" software

There is no such thing. The BIR registers how your business uses a
system, not a software brand, and its 14 September 2026 advisory says it
has not accredited any e-invoicing provider. You register Odoo as your
computerised books at your Revenue District Office, and we prepare the
samples, audit trail and system description with you.
